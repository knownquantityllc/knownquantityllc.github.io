How to play

  1. Monsters come in at the top and walk the shortest way to your camp at the bottom. The arrow shows each one's next step.
  2. Drag pieces onto the floor: they become walls, and monsters walk around them. Every piece is one turn, then the monsters move.
  3. Fill a row or a column and it crushes. Walls and monsters both count, so a monster standing in the last gap gets crushed. Two lines at once, or crushes on back-to-back turns, hit much harder.
  4. Leave them a way through. A piece that would wall them in turns red.
  5. Every monster that reaches your camp hits you. Beat the whole wave to clear the floor.
  6. Gems fire when their line is crushed: sword hits harder, heart heals, shield blocks, bomb blasts the 3×3 around it. Tap any monster to see what it does.
