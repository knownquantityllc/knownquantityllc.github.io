How to play

Slide sideways to move your mitt round your planet's sky. Touch the comet to catch it.
Holding it? Pull back anywhere and let go to throw. The dotted line shows where it flies.
Gravity bends every throw: curve round suns, bank off rocks, slip through wormholes.
Get it past their mitt and their planet pulls it in: a crater. First to 5 wins.
Catch a fast one dead centre: perfect! Your next throw is a supernova. Flick sideways as you let go to curve it. Don't hold on too long, it heats up.
