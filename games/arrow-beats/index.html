How to play

  1. Tap an arrow to send it off the board. It slides out the way its head points.
  2. Its path must be clear all the way to the edge. A blocked arrow bounces back and you lose a heart.
  3. Every arrow you clear plays the next note of the level's song. Clear them all and hear it play back.
  4. Finish with all 3 hearts for 3 stars. Stuck? A hint lights up an arrow that can go.
