How Mythnest works

🥚Pair any two creatures to make an egg. Each baby takes one of two hidden genes from each parent, so it can look like either parent, or a mix, or something neither of them shows.
🔥Elements fuse. Fire and water can make steam, earth and light can make crystal. There are 20 fusions to find.
⏳Eggs hatch in real time. Rarer eggs take longer. Tap an egg to keep it warm and it hatches sooner.
💖Stroke a creature to pet it, and feed it. Happy creatures give you glimmer, and parents with three hearts or more make luckier eggs.
✦Glimmer comes from care, goals, daily visits and releasing creatures. Spend it on charms and more nests.
🔁Trade with friends using a short code. No account or internet needed.
