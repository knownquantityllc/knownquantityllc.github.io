How to play

You're Mimi, a shape-shifting blob. Stay hidden until sunrise.

  • Tap the floor to sneak there. Mimi takes the stairs by itself.
  • Tap furniture to hide inside. Every use leaves goo smudges, and seekers check smudgy spots first, so mix it up.
  • Tap Mimi (or Mimic) to turn into a vase, lamp or plant. It burns goo, and it fails if someone watches you change.
  • Creaky boards and tattletale portraits make noise. Seekers come running.
  • The laundry chute drops you from the attic to the cellar in a flash. It's loud, but it washes off your scent.
  • Goo runs out while you hide or disguise, and you pop out when it's gone. It refills in the open, and cookies top it up (plus points and coins; golden ones are worth five).
