How to play

  • Drag anywhere to walk Wick around (or WASD / arrow keys).
  • Your weapon fires by itself at the closest Gloom creature.
  • Dash (Space) zips you forward and nothing can hurt you mid-dash.
  • Burst (E) blasts every Gloom near you. Learn it in the Forge.
  • Clear a room and the glowing gate opens at the top. Walk in to go deeper.
  • Beat the boss at the end of an area to unlock the next one.
  • Coins you pick up are yours to keep, even if the Gloom gets you. Spend them on new weapons and skills, or in any other Known Quantity game.
