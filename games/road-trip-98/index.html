How to play
Highway hands
Every leg of the trip is a little map. Each row and each column is a highway. Your card is on the dashboard and the next one is in the rear-view mirror. Tap a square to see what your card would make there, then tap it again to put it down (or drag the card onto the map). Cards stay where you put them.
Hands
A highway that fills up scores as a hand: a pair, two pair, three of a kind, a straight, a flush, a full house, four of a kind or a straight flush. Better hands drive more miles. Drive the leg's miles before the map is full to reach the next stop. Two and three stars are for driving further still.
On the map
Towns double both of their highways. Gas stations let you swap the next card. Road work closes a square. Speed traps fine highways that make less than two pair. Weather changes what pays.
Souvenirs
Every stop sends a postcard and a gift (card backs, tables and songs), and you pick a souvenir for the dashboard: up to five, each with its own bonus.
Car trouble?
If the map fills before you've driven far enough, try the leg again. The cards come in a different order every time, and the Hint button can help.
Daily Drive
One map for everybody, every day. Reach the goal to keep your streak going.
Motel card table
Klondike, Spider and FreeCell, for a rainy night: tap a card to send it to its best spot, or drag it. Undo is always free.
