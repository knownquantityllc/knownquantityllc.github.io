How to play

  • Swipe left / right (or ← →) to change lanes.
  • Swipe up (or ↑) to soar over things below you.
  • Swipe down (or ↓) to dive under things above you.
  • Fly through the splash rings to switch between sky and sea. Each switch raises your score multiplier.
  • Coins go into the shared Known Quantity profile, so you can spend them in any of our games.
  • Power-ups: magnet pulls coins in, bubble blocks one crash, x2 doubles coins.
