Privacy policy

How to play

  1. Every pad is in key. Low notes at the bottom, high at the top. Gold pads are the home note.
  2. Tap to play, slide across pads to glide, hold a pad for vibrato and wiggle your finger for more.
  3. Several fingers play chords on pianos, organs and strings.
  4. Backing gives you a drone or a slow chord loop in your key. Loop records what you play and repeats it, as many layers as you like.
  5. Pedals shape the sound: drive, wah, phaser, chorus, tremolo, delay, reverb, and a harmony pedal that adds a second voice in key.
  6. Song records a melody over four chords, in time. Save it, share its code, or play it as falling notes in Chordfall.
  7. Try a new scale for a new mood: blues, dorian, lydian, hirajoshi…
