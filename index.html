For parents

What the ages mean

  • Ages 4+Simple controls and little or no reading.
  • Ages 7+More reading or strategy, or mild cartoon action such as slapstick or cartoon monsters.
  • Ages 10+Lots of reading, harder rules, cartoon explosions, or optional online play with a friend.

These are our own recommendations based on each game's content and difficulty. They are not an official ESRB or PEGI rating.

In every game

  • No ads and no purchases
  • No chat and no accounts
  • We collect no personal data
  • Progress is saved only in this browser

Volley Valley is the only game with online play. It connects directly to a friend who shares an invite code, and it has no chat.

About the studio

Known Quantity LLC is a small independent studio that designs, builds and publishes its own games. Its mobile apps are released under the Gimble Gaming label.

Known Quantity LLC also runs two clothing stores: Matched by Masha, with new pieces, and Worn by Masha, with pre-loved clothing.

Every game is designed for phones first and built in-house with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The same code ships as apps for Android and iPhone.

  • OriginalEvery game, character and sound made in-house
  • Family friendlyNo ads, no purchases, no accounts
  • Cross-platformWeb, Android and iPhone from one codebase
  • Shared progressOur newest games share coins and high scores
